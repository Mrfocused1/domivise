Privacy Policy

Before you publish: this is a working draft, not legal advice. Have it reviewed against UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 by a qualified adviser, and update the company details once incorporation completes.

Who we are

DomiVise Ltd ("DomiVise", "we", "us") is building an AI-powered property management, landlord accounting and advisory platform for landlords and property teams. We are registered in England & Wales (company number pending). For any privacy question or request, contact hello@domivise.co.uk.

What we collect now

Through our early-access signup form we collect: your name, email address, approximate portfolio size, and — if you choose to share it — a description of your biggest property-management challenge. We also record which page element referred you to the form, the updates permission you selected and the version of this privacy notice shown when you joined.

Why we collect it

  • To manage Founding 100 membership and contact you about beta access and product progress;
  • To understand what features matter most to early users;
  • To send occasional product updates, which you can stop anytime via unsubscribe or by emailing us.

Our legal basis

We process this information based on your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw consent at any time — we will delete your details unless we must keep them by law.

Who gets access

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with service providers who help us run this site and communicate with you (form processing, email delivery), under contracts requiring them to protect it. When the platform launches, hosting is planned within the UK/EU.

How long we keep it

We keep signup details while DomiVise is in development and you remain interested in updates. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, we remove it promptly.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

Changes to this policy

As the product develops, this policy will be updated. Material changes will be announced to members by email and on this page with a new "last updated" date.
